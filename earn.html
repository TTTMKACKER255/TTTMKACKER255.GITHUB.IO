SKILLS FOR YOUR FUTURE

Learn Skills. Create Opportunities.

Explore useful digital skills and small creative projects. Progress takes practice, and income is never guaranteed.

🎨

Design

Practise making posters, thumbnails, presentations or graphics and build a portfolio.

🎬

Video Editing

Learn cuts, captions and vertical layouts using material you have permission to use.

🌐

Web Basics

Build simple pages with HTML and CSS, then test them on phones and computers.

📘

Digital Resources

Create original revision checklists, planners or guides that help a specific audience.

✍️

Writing & Research

Practise clear writing and fact-checking. Never pass off other people's work as your own.

🧩

Problem Solving

Find a small problem, plan a useful solution, test it and improve it.

A sensible first plan

  1. Choose one skill you enjoy.
  2. Practise and make a few examples.
  3. Ask a trusted adult before dealing with clients or payments.
  4. Check age requirements and platform rules; never pay to access a promised job.
